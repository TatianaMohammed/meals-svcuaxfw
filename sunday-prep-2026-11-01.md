# Sunday Prep — November 1, 2026 · 🫙 Glass-Dish Bake-Off

**Total time:** ~2.5 hrs (about 1 hr hands-on)
**Goal:** By dinner you'll have breakfasts done for the week, Birria casserole on the table, all Mon–Wed solo meals set up, and Thu lunches in the freezer.

**Oven order:** 425°F veg → 350°F pancakes → 375°F casserole.

---

## ✅ Before you start (Sat night)
- [ ] **Tonight:** move 1.5 lb ground beef → fridge (or cold-water thaw Sun AM, 1 hr)
- [ ] Check the GF labels on the birria, peanut, pesto and cilantro-lime sauces and the rice ramen
- [ ] Get out: 2 × 9×13, 2 × 8×8, 6 single oven-safe glass containers, lidded storage

---

## 1:00 PM — Veg in the oven (15 min hands-on)
- [ ] Heat the oven to **425°F**
- [ ] Peel rutabaga #1 and cut into ¾" cubes. Halve all the Brussels sprouts.
- [ ] Toss on 2 sheet pans with 2 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper, **1 tbsp rosemary + 1 tbsp marjoram** (garden)
- [ ] Roast 30–35 min, flipping halfway. Rutabaga should be golden and tender.
- [ ] **Rutabaga #2 stays raw** for the Thu lamb sheet pan

## 1:05 PM — Stovetop, at the same time
- [ ] **Quinoa:** 1 cup dry + 2 cups water. Simmer 15 min, rest 5, fluff, spread on a plate to cool → ~3 cups
- [ ] **Red lentils:** 1 cup rinsed + 2½ cups water. Simmer 12 min until just soft, then drain.
- [ ] **Hard-boiled eggs:** 4 eggs, 11 min, then into an ice bath (T snacks Sun/Tue/Thu/Sat)

## 1:20 PM — Pancake batters (10 min)
- [ ] **Blueberry-banana batter:** blend 2 scoops Ritual, 1 cup GF oats, 1 cup egg whites, 2 eggs, 6 oz cottage cheese, 1 banana, baking powder, cinnamon, vanilla
- [ ] **Pumpkin batter:** blend 2 scoops Ritual, 1 cup GF oats, 1 cup egg whites, 2 eggs, 1 cup pumpkin, ½ cup Greek yogurt, maple syrup, pumpkin spice, baking powder
- [ ] Pour each into a greased 8×8 and top the blueberry one with frozen blueberries

## 1:40 PM — Swap the oven
- [ ] Veg out → cool on the pans
- [ ] Drop the oven to **350°F**. Pancake bakes go in for **22–25 min**.

## 1:45 PM — Birria casserole assembly (20 min)
- [ ] Brown 1.5 lb ground beef and drain. Add 1 diced onion + 3 garlic cloves, 4 min.
- [ ] Stir in 1 cup birria sauce, the cooked lentils, 4 cups spinach and 2 tbsp lovage
- [ ] Spread in a greased 9×13 and top with 1 cup mozzarella
- [ ] Hold on the counter (no more than 30 min) or in the fridge until the oven's free

## 2:10 PM — Pancakes out, casserole in
- [ ] Pancakes out: cool 10 min, cut each into 6 squares
  - **Fridge:** 3 blueberry squares (T Sun–Tue) + 1 for K Sun
  - **Freezer:** 3 blueberry + all 6 pumpkin, wrapped one by one (T Wed–Sat, K Thu–Sat)
- [ ] Oven to **375°F**. Birria casserole in for **20–25 min**, or bake it closer to dinner if you'd rather eat it fresh. It reheats well either way.

## 2:15 PM — Rotisserie chicken + kits (20 min)
- [ ] Shred the whole rotisserie chicken. Skin and bones go in the trash or a freezer bag for broth.
  - **Sun L:** 10 oz (T 4 oz, K 6 oz)
  - **Wed peanut bake:** 10 oz → lidded container, fridge
  - Any extra → quick protein snack by Tue
- [ ] **Salmon kits (Mon):** in 2 single glass dishes, add ¼ cup quinoa + 1½ cups broccoli each. Lid and refrigerate. Salmon goes on top Monday.
- [ ] **Trout kits (Tue):** in 2 single glass dishes, add 1 cup roasted Brussels + ¾ cup roasted rutabaga each. Lid and refrigerate.
- [ ] Portion quinoa: ⅓ cup for T Sun L, ½ cup for K Sun L. **Freeze 1 cup** for the Fri lamb bowls.

## 2:35 PM — Sauces (10 min)
- [ ] **Dill-mint yogurt sauce** (¾ cup yogurt, dill, mint, lemon, garlic, salt) → jar
- [ ] **Lemon-lovage vinaigrette** (olive oil, lemon, Dijon, lovage, honey) → jar
- [ ] Wash + dry the romaine/greens and store with a paper towel

## 2:45 PM — Lunch! 🍗 Rotisserie chicken pesto bowls (both)
Chicken + quinoa + roasted Brussels + 1 tbsp red pepper almond pesto + greens

## ~6:00 PM — Dinner: Birria casserole 🌮
- [ ] Serve T 1 portion, K 1.5, topped with Greek yogurt + cilantro-lime + lime
- [ ] Once it's cool (within 2 hrs):
  - **Mon L (T):** 1 portion → fridge
  - **Thu L:** T 1 + K 1.5 → **freezer** (label "Thu 11/5")
  - **Spare:** 2 portions → freezer (bank)
