## 🍳 BREAKFASTS

### `gf-overnight-oats` — GF Berry Almond Overnight Oats
**5 min prep · 340 cal · 22g P**

- ½ cup **certified GF rolled oats** (Bob's Red Mill)
- ¾ cup unsweetened almond milk
- ½ cup 2% Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp chia · 1 tbsp ground flax
- ½ cup mixed berries · 1 tbsp walnuts (AM)
- ½ tsp cinnamon · vanilla

Combine everything except walnuts in mason jar. Refrigerate overnight (up to 4 days). Top with walnuts in AM.

### `gf-egg-scramble` — Spinach Feta Egg Scramble + GF Toast
**8 min · 330 cal · 25g P**

- 2 eggs + 1 egg white
- 1 cup baby spinach
- 1 tbsp feta · 1 tsp olive oil
- **1 slice Canyon Bakehouse GF bread**, toasted
- Salt, pepper, pinch turmeric

Wilt spinach in oil 30 sec. Pour whisked eggs. Scramble; add feta last 30 sec.

### `lentil-sausage-soup` — Lentil & Chicken Sausage Soup
**30 min · 4 servings · 435 cal · 32g P per serving**

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion diced · 3 garlic cloves minced
- 2 **GF chicken sausages**, sliced
- 1 cup dry lentils · 1 can (28 oz) diced tomatoes
- 4 cups **GF chicken broth** · 1 tsp cumin · ½ tsp turmeric · ½ tsp smoked paprika
- 2 cups chopped kale · lemon

1. Sauté onion 4 min; add garlic + sausage 3 min.
2. Add lentils, tomatoes, broth, spices. Simmer 25 min.
3. Stir in kale, cook 3 min. Squeeze lemon.
4. Portion into 4 containers. **Fridge 5 days, freezer 3 months.**

## 🍽️ DINNERS

### `sheet-pan-salmon` — Sheet Pan Salmon + Sweet Potato + Asparagus ⚡
**25 min · 2 servings · 470 cal · 36g P**

- 2 salmon fillets (5 oz each), skin on
- 1 bunch asparagus · 1 sweet potato diced ½"
- 2 tbsp olive oil · smoked paprika · turmeric · salt · pepper
- 1 lemon sliced

425°F. Roast sweet potato 12 min. Add salmon (oiled + spiced + lemon on top) + asparagus. Roast 12 more min. **Naturally GF.**

*Make double the salmon → Tue lunch.*

### `gf-turkey-meatballs` — GF Turkey Meatballs + Zoodles ⚡
**30 min · 4 servings · 435 cal · 38g P**

- 1 lb ground turkey 93/7
- 1 egg
- ⅓ cup grated Parmesan
- **3 tbsp almond flour** (instead of breadcrumbs)

### `ginger-salmon-bowl` — Ginger Salmon Rice Bowl ⚡ FAST
**15 min · 470 cal · 38g P**

- 5 oz salmon fillet, skin off
- Glaze: 1 tbsp **tamari** + 1 tsp honey + 1 tsp grated ginger + ½ tsp sesame oil
- ½ cup cooked jasmine rice
- 1 cup steamed broccoli or bok choy
- Green onion + sesame seeds

Brush salmon with glaze. Pan-sear medium-high, 3 min per side. Serve over rice with veg. **Uses tamari not soy sauce = GF.**

### `chicken-stir-fry-gf` — Chicken Stir-Fry + Rice Noodles ⚡
**15 min · 455 cal · 40g P**

- 5 oz chicken breast sliced thin
- 2 cups frozen stir-fry vegetables
- 1 tbsp avocado oil · 1 tbsp **tamari**
- 1 tsp ginger · 1 garlic clove · 1 tsp sesame oil
- 2 oz cooked **rice noodles** (not wheat noodles)

Wok on high with avocado oil. Stir-fry chicken 4 min. Add ginger + garlic 30 sec, then frozen veg 4 min. Toss with tamari + sesame oil. Serve over rice noodles.

### `turkey-chili` — Turkey Chili
**40 min · 6 servings · 465 cal · 38g P** (freezer gold)

- 1 tbsp olive oil · 1 onion + 1 bell pepper diced · 3 garlic cloves
- 1 lb ground turkey 93/7
- 2 tbsp **GF chili powder** · 1 tbsp cumin · 1 tsp smoked paprika · ½ tsp cinnamon
- 1 tbsp tomato paste · 1 can (28 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 can black beans · 1 can kidney beans
- 1 cup **GF chicken broth**
- Toppings: avocado · Greek yogurt · cilantro

Sauté onion + pepper 5 min. Add garlic 30 sec, turkey 5 min. Add spices + paste 1 min. Add tomatoes, beans, broth. Simmer 25 min. **6 portions, freeze 3.** Naturally GF.

---

### `roasted-chicken-thighs` — Herb Roasted Chicken Thighs + Brussels + Sweet Potato
**45 min mostly hands-off · 4 servings · 485 cal · 42g P**

- 4 bone-in skin-on chicken thighs
- 1.5 lbs Brussels halved · 2 sweet potatoes diced
- 3 tbsp olive oil · za'atar · garlic powder · salt · pepper
- 1 lemon

425°F. Toss veg with oil. Rub chicken with oil + spices. All on 2 sheet pans, roast 35–40 min until 175°F. Squeeze lemon.

**Save extra chicken meat → next week's Med jars.** Naturally GF.
