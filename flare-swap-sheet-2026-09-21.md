## 🚨 Rule of thumb during flare
- **Cooked > raw** (spinach sautéed, tomato roasted, jalapeño roasted only)
- **Well-braised > seared** (stew > steak)
- **Blended > chunky** (soup > salad)
- **Broth-forward** (sip bone broth 1 cup 1–2×/day)
- **Skip:** raw greens, raw jalapeño/serrano, nuts+skins if severe, popcorn/crumbs, sausages, alcohol, caffeine spike
- **Add:** ginger tea, turmeric, cooked lentils, cod, chicken thigh, sweet potato, butternut, cooked spinach

## 🧡 Anti-inflam pantry cheat sheet

**Always safe during flare:**
- Bone broth, ginger tea, turmeric golden milk
- Cottage cheese, Greek yogurt (probiotics — if tolerated)
- Well-cooked white rice, basmati, quinoa (rinsed)
- Roasted sweet potato, roasted butternut squash
- Cooked spinach, cooked carrot
- Cod, poached chicken thigh
- Well-cooked lentils (dal)
- Ripe banana, applesauce, cooked apple

**Add for anti-inflam boost:**
- Ginger (fresh, tea, grated)
- Turmeric (pinch in everything)
- Chia + flax (soaked)
- Honey (small drizzle)
- Coconut milk (light)
- Lemon (small squeeze)

**Skip during flare:**
- Raw veg (especially cruciferous, jalapeño, onion)
- Popcorn, seeds w/ shells
- Nuts (if severe — else small amount of AB is OK)
- Sausages, cured meats, processed meat
- Fried foods
- Alcohol
- Big caffeine dose
- High-fat dairy blocks (small yogurt/cottage cheese OK)

## 🚨 Escalation signals — call OB/GI

- Blood in stool
- Persistent >7 BMs/day
- Severe cramping unmanaged
- Fever
- Weight loss over 5 lb in a week
- Any TTC-specific worry (cramping + spotting)

**You're currently OFF mesalamine — pharmacy follow-up = priority 1.** If not already, call to refill/find alternative.
