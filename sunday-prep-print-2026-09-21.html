10:15 Sun · START FIRST
🍲 Bone broth 🧡 (2–3 hr simmer, background)
  • Large pot: chicken thigh trim (save from poach) OR 1 lb chicken backs
  • Add 2 carrots chopped, 1 onion halved, 4 smashed garlic
  • Add 2" ginger sliced, 1 tsp turmeric, peppercorns, bay leaves, thyme, salt
  • Cover w/ 8 cups water, boil, reduce to bare simmer
  • Skim foam occasionally
  • Strain after 2–3 hr → portion: 2c stew · 2c soup · 2c freezer bags
10:30 Sun
🍗 Chicken thigh poach (2 lb, 25 min)
  • Salt water + bay + thyme + garlic in medium pot
  • Add 2 lb thighs (bone-in preferred — pull bones/skin into broth pot)
  • Gentle simmer 25 min until 165°F
  • Cool, shred large chunks
  • Portion 4 containers: 1.5c curry batch · 1c Tue soup topper · 1c Tue snack box (drizzle ginger-miso before packing Mon pm) · 1c Wed L bowl
11:00 Sun
🥔 Oven sheet-pan batch @ 400°F (parallel / rotate)
  • Sheet 1: 3 large sweet potatoes cubed + olive oil + salt. 35 min. → Sun D + Tue L + Fri B hash + Sat D
  • Sheet 2: 6 romas quartered + 2 jalapeños halved seeded + 4 garlic + olive oil + salt. 30 min. Pulse loosely w/ lime + cilantro → chunky salsa base. → Fri D + snacks
  • Sheet 3: 2 lb roasted chicken thighs (thyme + garlic + salt). 35 min at 400°F. → Fri L + Sat L + snack meat
  • Sheet 4: butternut squash halves cut-side down + olive oil + salt. 40 min. Scoop flesh → into soup pot
11:45 Sun
🍲 Ginger butternut squash soup 🧡🧡 (~30 min after squash)
  • Sauté 1 diced onion in olive oil 5 min
  • Bloom 3 garlic + 2" grated ginger + 1 tsp turmeric + 2 thyme sprigs 1 min
  • Add roasted butternut flesh + 3 cups bone broth
  • Simmer 10 min. Blend silky w/ immersion blender
  • Stir in ½ cup coconut milk. Salt + pepper
  • Portion: 4 cups → Tue L + S2 backup container · 2 cups → freezer bags
12:15 Sun
🍲 Red lentil dal 🧡 (35 min)
  • Sauté 1 diced onion in olive oil 5 min
  • Bloom 4 garlic + 2" ginger + 1 minced seeded jalapeño + 1 tsp each turmeric/cumin/coriander/garam masala, 1 min
  • Add 1 can diced tomato OR 2 diced romas, 2 min
  • Add 1.5 cups rinsed red lentils + 4 cups water. Simmer 20–25 min until collapsed
  • Salt heavily. Finish w/ lemon + fresh dill or cilantro
  • Serve Sun D over basmati + wilted spinach. Leftover → Mon L container
12:50 Sun
🥥 Coconut chicken curry 🧡 (~25 min)
  • Sauté 1 diced onion 5 min
  • Bloom 2 garlic + 1.5" ginger + 1 tsp turmeric 1 min
  • Add 1 sliced bell pepper 3 min
  • Add 6–8 coconut curry cubes + 1 can light coconut milk, whisk to melt
  • Add 1.5 cups shredded thigh, simmer 10 min
  • Wilt 2 cups spinach at end. Finish w/ basil
  • Portion: Mon D (2 serv) · 1 freezer · 1 backup
1:15 Sun
🍳 Cottage cheese egg bake (30 min bake @ 375°F)
  • Whisk 8 eggs + 1 cup cottage cheese + salt + pepper
  • Fold in 2 cups chopped spinach + ½ cup parm + green onion + basil + 2 garlic
  • Pour into greased 9x13, bake 375°F 30 min
  • Cool 5 min, slice into 8 → labeled container. Mon–Wed B.
1:50 Sun
🥩 Meatball batch (18 min bake @ 400°F, 24 balls)
  • Combine 1.5 lb ground beef + 1 egg + ¼ cup GF breadcrumbs (or crushed rice cracker) + 3 garlic + ¼ cup parm + parsley + salt + pepper
  • Roll ~1.5" balls (~24 total)
  • Bake 400°F 18 min on lined sheet
  • Cool. 12 → Fri D container. 12 → freezer bag flat single layer first.
