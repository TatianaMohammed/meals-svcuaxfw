- 3 tbsp fresh key lime juice (~4–5 key limes)

- 1 tsp Dijon

## 🐟 MON — Sheet-Pan Salmon (batch 4)

**Feeds Mon D both + Tue L both. 40 min, 10 min hands-on.**

- 4 salmon portions (~6 oz each)
- 2 sweet potatoes, ½" cubes
- 1 head broccoli, florets
- 3 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper
- **Fresh thyme + marjoram** (garden, 1 tbsp each)
- 1 lemon, sliced

1. 425°F. Toss sweet potato + oil + salt on sheet → roast 15 min.
2. Add broccoli + more oil. Push to sides, add salmon.
3. Top salmon with herbs + lemon + salt. Roast 12–14 min.
4. Serve 2 with rice on side (Khalid extra). Save 2 salmon + rice for Tue L bowls.

## 🌭 TUE — Mediterranean Beef Sausage Sheet Pan (uses 4 sausages)

**25–30 min total, 10 min hands-on.**

- 4 beef sausages (freezer — thaw Mon night)
- 3 bell peppers (mixed colors), sliced
- 1 red onion, wedged
- 1 lb baby potatoes, halved (from HR floating stash or bag)
- 3 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper
- 1 tsp oregano · 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ cup kalamata olives (pantry)
- **Fresh marjoram + basil** to finish
- Lemon wedges

1. 425°F. Toss potatoes with oil + salt + paprika → sheet pan, roast 15 min.
2. Add peppers + onion + oregano, toss.
3. Nestle sausages on top. Roast 20–25 min until sausages cooked through + potatoes crisp.
4. Scatter olives + marjoram + basil. Squeeze lemon.

*Save 1 sausage + veg for Wed L bowl.*

## 🐟 WED — Key Lime Baked Cod (2 portions)

**25 min, 10 min hands-on.**

- 2 cod portions (~6 oz each)
- Zest + juice of 2 key limes
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed
- 1 cup quinoa (Sun prep)
- Salt, pepper
- **Fresh basil + marjoram** (garden, chopped)

1. 400°F. Toss asparagus with oil + salt → roast 8 min.
2. Whisk key lime juice + zest + garlic + olive oil + salt.
3. Push asparagus aside, add cod. Spoon key lime mix over cod.
4. Roast 10–12 min until cod flakes.
5. Warm quinoa, toss with basil. Plate cod on top, marjoram to finish.

## 🌶️ THU — Beef Chili (batch 4)

**Feeds Thu D both + Fri L both + freeze extra. 35 min.**

- 1 lb 90% lean ground beef (freezer thaw Wed pm)
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 serrano, minced (or jalapeño)
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tsp cumin · 1 tsp smoked paprika · 1 tsp oregano · ½ tsp cinnamon
- 1 can diced tomatoes (14 oz)
- 1 can kidney beans, drained
- 1 can black beans, drained
- 1 cup broth (or chicken stock if any left)
- Salt, pepper
- Toppings: avocado, Greek yogurt, cilantro, cheese (Khalid)

1. Brown beef, drain excess fat.
2. Add onion + pepper + serrano + garlic, cook 5 min.
3. Add spices, stir 30 sec.
4. Add tomatoes, beans, broth. Simmer 20 min.
5. Salt to taste. Portion: 2 servings tonight, 2 for Fri L (fridge), any extra to freezer.

## 🌭 FRI — Sausage & Peppers Skillet (uses 4 sausages)

**25 min. Feeds both, freeze remaining 4 sausages.**

- 4 beef sausages (freezer — thaw Thu pm)
- 3 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 red onion, sliced
- 4 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp fennel seed (if have) · red pepper flakes
- Splash chicken/veg broth
- **Fresh basil** to finish
- Rice or serve as-is

1. Brown sausages in skillet 5 min per side. Remove.
2. Same pan: peppers + onion + garlic + olive oil, cook 8 min.
3. Add fennel + flakes + broth splash. Return sausages, simmer 5 min covered.
4. Slice sausages, plate over rice (Khalid extra) or naked (Tati). Top with basil + parm.

## 🫘 SAT — Moong Dal (pantry — batch 3)

**Feeds Sat D + Sun L + 1 spare. 40 min.**

- 1 cup moong beans, rinsed (soaked 2 hrs helps)
- 3 cups water or broth
- 1 small onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp ginger, grated
- 2 tsp cumin · 1 tsp turmeric · 1 tsp coriander
- ½ tsp cayenne
- 1 tbsp ghee or coconut oil
- Salt
- Basmati rice on side
- **Fresh basil or cilantro** to finish

1. Sauté onion + garlic + ginger + spices in ghee 3 min.
2. Add moong + water/broth, simmer 25–30 min until soft.
3. Salt. Serve with rice + soft-boiled egg.

## 🍗 SUN — Roasted Chicken Legs (batch 4, from freezer)

**Feeds Sun D + 2 shred for next week.**

- 4 frozen chicken legs (thaw in fridge Sat)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- **Fresh thyme + marjoram** (1 tbsp each)
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder
- 2 sweet potatoes, cubed
- Salad greens + Key Lime Vinaigrette

1. 425°F. Rub chicken with oil + herbs + salt/pepper/garlic powder.
2. Sheet pan: chicken + sweet potato.
3. Roast 40 min until skin crisp and internal 175°F.
4. Eat 2, shred 2 into container for next week.
