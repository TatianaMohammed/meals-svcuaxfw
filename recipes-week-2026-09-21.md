## 🥣 SUNDAY BATCH RECIPES (all made Sun morning/early afternoon)

### 🍲 Ginger Bone Broth (base for stew + soup) — ~6 cups
**Ingredients:**
- Chicken thigh trim (skin, bones, scraps) OR use whole thighs and pull meat after
- 2 carrots, roughly chopped
- 1 onion (halved, skin on)
- 4 cloves garlic (smashed)
- 2" ginger, sliced 🧡
- 1 tsp turmeric 🧡
- 1 tsp black peppercorns
- 2 bay leaves
- 3 sprigs thyme
- Salt to taste
- 8 cups water

**Method:**
1. Combine all in large pot. Bring to boil, reduce to bare simmer.
2. Simmer 2–3 hours, skimming foam.
3. Strain, discard solids. Cool.
4. Portion: 2 cups → stew Wed · 2 cups → soup Sun · 2 cups → freezer.

**Note:** Anti-inflam gold. Sip warm mug during flare — soothes gut lining. Freezes 3 months.

### 🍲 Ginger Butternut Squash Soup 🧡🧡 (Sun batch, ~6 cups, ~180 cal/cup + 4g P)
**Ingredients:**
- 1 whole butternut squash, halved + seeded
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2" ginger, grated 🧡
- 1 tsp turmeric 🧡
- 2 sprigs thyme
- 3 cups bone broth (from batch above)
- ½ cup coconut milk (from a can — light) OR splash cream
- Salt, pepper, drizzle olive oil

**Method:**
1. Roast squash halves cut-side down at 400°F, 40 min until fork-tender. Scoop flesh.
2. Sauté onion in olive oil 5 min. Add garlic + ginger + turmeric + thyme, 1 min.
3. Add squash flesh + broth. Simmer 10 min.
4. Blend (immersion or upright) until silky. Stir in coconut milk. Season.
5. Portion: 4 cups → labeled container (Tue L + S2 backup) · 2 cups → freezer bags.

**Serving Tue L:** 1.5 cups soup + stirred-in shredded poached chicken thigh (~4 oz) + rice crackers on side. **460 cal / 32g P.**

### 🍲 Red Lentil Dal 🧡 (Sun D + Mon L, ~4 servings, ~360 cal / 20g P + 12g fiber)
**Ingredients:**
- 1.5 cups rinsed red lentils (⚠️ use this week!)
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic
- 2" ginger, grated 🧡
- 1 jalapeño, seeded, minced (COOKED — flare-safe once cooked)
- 1 tsp turmeric 🧡
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 can diced tomato OR 2 romas, diced
- 4 cups water or broth
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Fresh dill or cilantro to finish
- Olive oil or ghee

**Method:**
1. Heat oil, sauté onion 5 min. Add garlic + ginger + jalapeño + spices, 1 min bloom.
2. Add tomato, cook 2 min.
3. Add lentils + water. Bring to boil, reduce, simmer 20–25 min until lentils collapse.
4. Season heavily. Finish w/ lemon + fresh herbs.
5. Serve over basmati rice + wilted spinach.

**Note:** Turmeric + ginger + well-cooked lentil = flare-friendly. Freezes well. TTC-safe.

### 🥥 Coconut Chicken Curry 🧡 (Sun batch, 4 servings, ~450 cal / 38g P)
**Ingredients:**
- 2 lb poached chicken thighs (shredded, from Sun batch)
- Coconut curry sauce cubes (from freezer — 6–8 cubes)
- 1 can light coconut milk OR ½ block coconut cream
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1.5" ginger, grated 🧡
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 2 cups baby spinach
- 1 tsp turmeric 🧡
- Fresh basil to finish
- Basmati rice to serve

**Method:**
1. Sauté onion 5 min. Add garlic + ginger + turmeric, 1 min.
2. Add bell pepper, 3 min. Add curry cubes + coconut milk, whisk.
3. Add shredded chicken. Simmer 10 min.
4. Fold in spinach at end, wilt.
5. Finish w/ basil.

**Portion:** 4 servings — Mon D (T+K, 2 servings), 1 freezer, 1 backup lunch (Wed L → freezer since Wed L moved to bowl).

### 🍲 Beef Stew over Quinoa (Wed D, 6 servings → 3 tonight + 3 freezer, ~450 cal / 38g P per serv)
**Ingredients:**
- 2 lb beef cubes (dredged in ~2 tbsp GF flour or cornstarch)
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 carrots, chunked
- 4 cloves garlic
- 2" ginger, grated 🧡
- 2 cups bone broth (Sun batch)
- 1 can diced tomato OR 3 romas chopped
- 2 sprigs rosemary
- 3 sprigs thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- Splash red wine (optional)
- Quinoa base to serve

**Method:**
1. Sear beef in olive oil, batches, until browned. Remove.
2. Sauté onion + carrot 5 min. Garlic + ginger 1 min.
3. Return beef. Add broth + tomato + herbs. Bring to simmer.
4. Cover, low heat 2 hours (or 6 hrs slow cooker). Beef should fall apart.
5. Adjust salt, remove herb stems + bay.
6. Serve over quinoa. Portion 3 servings → freezer.

**Note:** Bone broth base + well-braised beef + ginger = flare-safe. Rosemary + thyme classic.

### 🐟 Miso-Ginger Cod + Roasted Eggplant + Quinoa (Thu D, 2 serv, ~470 cal / 40g P)
**Ingredients:**
- 2 cod portions (thawed — pull Mon night)
- 2 tbsp brown miso + 1 tbsp tamari + 1" ginger grated + 1 tsp honey + 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 eggplant, ½" rounds
- 2 servings quinoa
- Green onion + sesame seeds to finish

**Method:**
1. Whisk miso + tamari + ginger + honey + sesame oil.
2. Marinate cod 20 min.
3. Toss eggplant rounds w/ olive oil + salt. Sheet pan, 400°F, 15 min.
4. Push eggplant to one side. Add cod, brush w/ marinade. Roast 10 more min.
5. Serve over quinoa. Top w/ green onion + sesame.

**Note:** Omega-3 anti-inflam reset. TTC-safe (cod low mercury).
