### Lemon-Garlic Salmon Rice Bowl (Wed) ⭐ mid-week swap
**12 min · 510 cal · 38g P**
- 1 portion fresh salmon (last one — must go Wed per 2-day fridge rule)
- ~1 cup leftover rice from Sat (day 4 — last safe day)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced · 1 tbsp olive oil · 1 lemon (zest + juice)
- Big handful spinach · ½ bell pepper, diced · handful cherry tomatoes, halved
- Splash tamari · salt/pep · 1–2 tbsp grated parm

1. Pan-sear salmon in ½ tbsp olive oil, 3–4 min per side. Rest, then flake.
2. Same pan: ½ tbsp oil + garlic + diced pepper, 2 min. Add rice, break up clumps, sauté 3 min until it crisps a bit.
3. Add spinach + cherry tomatoes + tamari, toss until spinach wilts (1 min).
4. Off heat: fold in flaked salmon + lemon zest + juice. Top w/ parm.

### Beyond Meat Stuffed Bell Peppers (Wed — batch 3, eat 1, freeze 2)
**35 min · 420 cal · 28g P per serving**
- 3 large bell peppers (halved lengthwise, seeded)
- 1 pkg Beyond Meat crumbles (or crumbled patties) · 1 tbsp olive oil
- ½ onion diced · 2 garlic cloves · ¾ cup cooked quinoa · ½ cup marinara or diced tomatoes
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning · salt/pep · ¼ cup Parmesan

1. Preheat 375°F. Arrange pepper halves in 9x13 dish w/ ¼ cup water.
2. Sauté onion 3 min, add garlic 30 sec, add Beyond Meat + seasoning, brown 5 min.
3. Stir in quinoa + marinara. Taste + salt.
4. Stuff pepper halves. Top w/ Parm.
5. Cover w/ foil, bake 25 min. Uncover, bake 5 min more.

**Freeze 4 halves** in individual containers for next week.

### Beef Stew w/ Chicken Stock (Sat — batch 3 servings)
**60 min mostly hands-off · 500 cal · 42g P**
- 1 lb beef cubes · 2 tbsp olive oil · salt/pep · 1 tbsp flour or GF starch
- 1 onion diced · 3 garlic · 3 carrots chunked · 1 sweet potato cubed · 1 bell pepper
- 4 cups **homemade chicken stock** (batched today) · 1 tbsp tomato paste · 1 tsp thyme · 1 bay leaf
- Fresh cilantro or parsley

1. Pat beef dry, salt/pep, dust w/ flour.
2. Heat oil in Dutch oven med-high. Sear beef 4 min per side. Remove.
3. Sauté onion 5 min, add garlic + tomato paste 1 min.
4. Return beef. Add stock + thyme + bay + veg. Simmer covered 45 min until beef tender.
5. Salt to taste. Top w/ herbs.
