## SUNDAY PREP RECIPES

### Roasted Squashes & Eggplant (Sun afternoon, ~1 hr total)

**Ingredients:**
- 1 small butternut squash (quarter or eighth it, skin OK for roasting)
- 1 small honey nut squash (halve it)
- 1 small delicata squash (slice into rings, seeds out)
- 1 small spaghetti squash (halve lengthwise, seeds out)
- 1 medium eggplant (cut into 1" cubes or batons)
- 3 tbsp ghee or olive oil
- Sea salt, black pepper, dried thyme
- Optional: garlic powder, cumin

**Instructions:**
1. Heat oven to 400°F on convection (or 425°F static).
2. Toss all veg in ghee/oil, salt, pepper, thyme on two sheet pans.
3. Roast 40–50 min, stirring halfway, until golden and fork-tender.
4. Cool fully before portioning into containers. Label & date.

**Yield:** ~6–7 cups total roasted veg, enough for 7 days + lunches.

**Storage:** Fridge 4–5 days in airtight containers. Freeze portions if making extra.

### Red Lentil Base (Sun afternoon, ~30 min)

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup red lentils (dry)
- 3 cups water or vegetable broth (if available)
- ½ tsp turmeric (optional, adds color + anti-inflammatory)
- Salt, pepper to taste
- 1 tbsp ghee or olive oil
- Optional: ¼ tsp cumin

**Instructions:**
1. Rinse lentils in a fine-mesh strainer.
2. Bring water/broth to boil in a pot. Add lentils and turmeric.
3. Reduce heat to low, simmer 20–25 min until lentils are fully soft and beginning to break down (paste-like, not grainy).
4. Stir in ghee/oil, salt, pepper. Taste and adjust.
5. Cool in container.

**Yield:** ~2 cups cooked (about 5–6 servings as a base).

**Macros per ½ cup:** ~70 kcal, 5g protein, 1g fat, 12g carb, 2g fiber.

**Storage:** Fridge 5 days. Freeze portions in ice-cube trays or 4 oz containers for Wed–Fri lunches.

### Quinoa Batch (Sun afternoon, ~20 min)

**Ingredients:**
- 1.5 cups quinoa (dry)
- 3 cups water or broth
- Pinch salt

**Instructions:**
1. Rinse quinoa in fine-mesh strainer under cold water.
2. Bring water to boil, add salt. Stir in quinoa.
3. Reduce heat to low, cover, simmer 15 min.
4. Remove from heat, fluff with fork. Let sit 5 min uncovered.
5. Spread on sheet pan to cool quickly (prevents mushiness).

**Yield:** ~3 cups cooked (about 6 servings).

**Macros per ½ cup:** ~85 kcal, 3.1g protein, 1.6g fat, 15g carb, 1.4g fiber.

**Storage:** Fridge 5 days. Freeze 4 oz portions by Wed if not used.

### Pan-Seared Paneer (Sun afternoon, ~10 min)

**Ingredients:**
- 8 oz paneer (from fridge, ~1.5" thick slab, or precut cubes)
- 1 tbsp ghee
- Sea salt, black pepper
- Optional: fresh cilantro, squeeze lemon after cooking

**Instructions:**
1. Pat paneer dry with paper towel (removes moisture, improves sear).
2. If using slab, cut into 1" cubes or 1/2" slices.
3. Heat ghee in a cast-iron or nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmer.
4. Working in batches, sear paneer 2–3 min per side until golden and crispy edges.
5. Cool on a plate. Sprinkle with cilantro + lemon if using.

**Yield:** 8 oz paneer = ~4 servings (2 oz per serving as protein component in a basket).

**Macros per 2 oz (1 serving):** ~80 kcal, 10g protein, 4g fat, 1g carb, 0g fiber.

**Storage:** Fridge 3–4 days in airtight container. Do NOT freeze.

## DINNER RECIPES (Prep Sunday; cook same-day evening)

### Black Cod — Pan-Seared (Sun D, ~12 min)

**Ingredients (2 portions):**
- 1 black cod fillet (~6–8 oz), thawed overnight fridge
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Sea salt, black pepper
- Juice ½ lemon + zest
- Fresh dill (from ahi package or garden)

**Instructions:**
1. Pat fish dry.
2. Heat oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
3. Season fish with salt, pepper.
4. Sear 4–5 min skin-side down (if skin on) until golden; flip, sear 2–3 min until cooked through (internal temp 145°F).
5. Plate, drizzle lemon juice, top fresh dill.

**Yield:** 2 portions (4–6 oz each).

**Macros per 5 oz cooked:** ~180 kcal, 22g protein, 9g fat, 0g carb, 0g fiber. High omega-3 (DHA + EPA).

**TTC:** ✅ Black cod = low mercury, TTC-safe for Tatiana.

**Serving:** Pair with roasted delicata squash + sautéed greens (see below).

### Sautéed Greens (Sun D side, ~5 min per 2 cups greens)

**Ingredients:**
- 2–3 cups fresh greens (spinach, romaine, mixed — TBD by grocery)
- 1 tbsp olive oil or ghee
- 1 garlic clove, minced (or ½ tsp garlic powder)
- Pinch salt, pepper
- Optional squeeze lemon, red pepper flakes

**Instructions:**
1. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
2. Add garlic, sauté 30 sec (don't burn).
3. Add greens in batches, stirring, until wilted (2–3 min total).
4. Season with salt, pepper, optional lemon.

**Yield:** ~1 cup cooked (reduces ~80% by volume).

**Macros per cup cooked:** ~40 kcal, 2g protein, 3.5g fat, 3g carb, 1g fiber.

**Prep-ahead note:** Wash & chop Sunday; store in sealed container. Sauté fresh Mon–Fri per dinner assembly.

### Salmon Fillet — Baked (Mon D, ~15 min)

**Ingredients (2 portions):**
- 1 salmon fillet (~6–8 oz), thawed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Sea salt, black pepper
- Fresh lemon + thyme (from garden or dried)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Line baking sheet with parchment.
3. Place salmon skin-side down, drizzle oil, season salt + pepper + thyme.
4. Bake 12–15 min until opaque and flakes with a fork (internal temp 145°F).
5. Plate, squeeze lemon.

**Yield:** 2 portions (4–6 oz each).

**Macros per 5 oz:** ~200 kcal, 24g protein, 10g fat, 0g carb, 0g fiber. High DHA (TTC-optimal).

**TTC:** ✅ Salmon = low mercury, TTC-safe + high omega-3.

**Serving:** Pair with roasted honey nut squash + sautéed greens.

### Ahi Tuna Steaks — Seared (Tue D, ~8 min) — **Khalid only**

**Ingredients (K: 1–2 steaks, ~6–8 oz total):**
- 1–2 ahi tuna steaks (~3–4 oz each), from freezer
- 1 tbsp olive oil or sesame oil
- Sea salt, black pepper
- Fresh dill (from ahi package)
- Optional: squeeze lemon, touch of soy sauce (if GF)

**Instructions:**
1. Remove ahi from freezer 20 min before cooking to slightly soften (still cold, not fully thawed = better sear).

3. Pat steaks dry with paper towel. Season salt, pepper.
4. Sear 2–3 min per side for medium-rare (ahi is best cooked rare to medium-rare; longer = dry).
5. Plate immediately, top fresh dill + lemon squeeze.

**Yield:** 1–2 steaks (6–8 oz total for K).

**Macros per 6 oz cooked:** ~200 kcal, 28g protein, 8g fat, 0g carb, 0g fiber.

### Ribeye Steak — Seared (Wed D, ~10 min)

**Ingredients (1 of 2 ribeyes, ~6–8 oz):**
- 1 ribeye steak (~6–8 oz), from freezer (thaw Tue evening fridge)
- Sea salt, black pepper
- ½ tbsp ghee or butter
- Fresh thyme sprig (garden or dried)
- Lemon wedge

**Instructions:**
1. Remove steak from fridge 20–30 min before cooking (room temp = even sear).
2. Pat dry thoroughly.
3. Heat cast-iron skillet over high heat until VERY hot (touch water droplet = should sizzle + evaporate).
4. Season steak generously with salt, pepper.
5. Sear 3–4 min per side (for medium-rare, 130–135°F internal). Do NOT move steak while searing.
6. In final 30 sec, add thyme sprig + ghee, tilt pan, baste steak with foaming butter.
7. Rest 5 min on plate before serving. Squeeze lemon.

**Yield:** 2 portions (4–6 oz per person after cooking loss).

**Macros per 5 oz cooked:** ~320 kcal, 28g protein, 22g fat, 0g carb, 0g fiber. High iron, B12.

**Serving:** Pair with roasted veg (butternut, delicata) + sautéed greens + optional squeeze lemon.

### Roasted Chicken Quarters (Thu D, ~45–60 min)

**Ingredients (4 quarters, ~2–2.5 lbs total):**
- 4 chicken quarters (thawed Wed evening fridge), from frozen 4 lb in inventory
- 2 tbsp ghee or olive oil
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard or soy-ginger paste (if on hand)
- 1 lb carrots, cut into 1–2" batons
- Sea salt, black pepper, dried thyme or rosemary
- 1 onion, quartered (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. Pat chicken dry. Rub with oil, mustard (if using), salt, pepper, thyme on all sides.
3. Arrange chicken skin-side up on a large sheet pan. Scatter carrots + onion around.
4. Roast 45–55 min until skin is golden and internal temp reaches 165°F (at thickest part, not touching bone).
5. Cool 10 min. Separate 2 cooked portions (Thu D for both) → keep hot. Cool remaining 2 quarters, refrigerate for Fri L basket.

**Yield:** 4 portions total (2 Thu D + 2 Fri L).

**Macros per 4.5 oz cooked (1 quarter): ~350 kcal, 35g protein, 20g fat, 0g carb, 0g fiber.**

**Serving:** Thu D = both eat 1 quarter each + roasted carrots + sautéed greens. Fri L = each gets 1 leftover quarter (cold or reheated) + roasted squash basket + greens.

### Ground Beef Braise (Fri D, ~25 min)

**Ingredients (2 portions):**
- ½ lb ground beef (~8 oz)
- 1 tbsp olive oil or ghee
- 1 small onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ small butternut or delicata squash (or roasted leftover), cubed (~1.5 cups)
- ¼ cup crushed tomatoes (from pantry) or tomato paste + water
- Sea salt, pepper, dried basil or oregano
- Optional: splash balsamic vinegar or Worcestershire

**Instructions:**
1. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Brown ground beef 5 min, breaking into small pieces. Drain excess fat if needed.
3. Add onion, cook 2 min until softened. Add garlic, cook 1 min.
4. Stir in squash, crushed tomatoes, salt, pepper, herbs.
5. Simmer 15 min until squash softens and flavors meld.
6. Taste, adjust salt/pepper. Optional: add splash balsamic.

**Yield:** 2 portions (can freeze 1 for next week if desired).

**Macros per portion (~½ lb cooked): ~280 kcal, 24g protein, 16g fat, 9g carb, 2g fiber.**

**Serving:** Pair with sautéed greens + optional quinoa or lentil base (if leftover). Both T + K eat same dinner.

### Chuck Roast Braise (Sat D, slow cooker or oven, ~3–4 hrs)

**Ingredients (2 portions + potential leftover):**
- 1 chuck roast (from freezer, thawed Fri fridge) — assume ~2–3 lbs
- 2 tbsp olive oil or ghee
- 1 onion, quartered
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- 2 cups carrots + other root veg (if available; from pantry or roasted Sunday)
- 1 cup beef or vegetable broth (or water)
- 2 bay leaves, 2 thyme sprigs (or 1 tsp dried thyme)
- Sea salt, pepper

**Instructions (Slow cooker method — preferred for Sat all-day):**
1. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear roast 3–4 min per side until browned. Transfer to slow cooker.
2. Add onion, garlic, carrots, broth, bay leaves, thyme, salt, pepper.
3. Cook on LOW 8 hrs or HIGH 4 hrs.
4. Remove from liquid. Shred or slice meat. Serve with braising liquid + cooked veg.

**Instructions (Oven method — if preference):**
1. Preheat oven to 325°F.
2. Sear roast in Dutch oven as above.
3. Add remaining ingredients, cover with lid.
4. Braise in oven 3–3.5 hrs until fork-tender.

**Yield:** 2–3 portions (roast yields ~1.5x its raw weight after braising; leftover can freeze).

**Macros per 5 oz cooked: ~360 kcal, 32g protein, 24g fat, 2g carb, 0g fiber. Iron, B12, zinc.**

**Serving:** Both T + K enjoy braise + braised veg + optional sautéed greens.

## OPTIONAL: Banana Muffins (no blueberries)

**Ingredients (makes 12 muffins):**
- ¾ cup almond flour
- ¾ cup certified GF oat flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ¾ tsp cinnamon
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp ginger
- 2 medium mashed bananas (~1 cup)
- ¼ cup honey
- ⅓ cup cottage cheese (pineapple OK)
- ¼ cup smooth peanut butter
- ¼ cup almond or reduced-fat milk
- 1 large egg
- 1.5 tsp vanilla
- Cooking spray

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease 12-cup muffin tin.
2. Whisk together flours, baking soda, baking powder, cinnamon, salt, ginger in a bowl.
3. In another bowl, beat banana, honey, cottage cheese, peanut butter, milk, egg, vanilla until smooth.
4. Fold wet into dry until just combined. Omit blueberries.
5. Divide evenly among 12 cups (batter will be less than original recipe).
6. Bake 16–20 min, checking early; a tester should come out clean.
7. Cool 5 min in pan, turn out to cool completely.
8. Freeze extra muffins in an airtight bag.

**Yield:** 12 muffins (3.5 oz each).

**Macros per muffin:** ~145 kcal, 25g carb, 4.8g protein, 4g fat, 2.4g fiber.

**Use:** Tue B + future breakfasts (Tatiana). Khalid prefers eggs; muffin = optional snack.

**Storage:** Fridge 4 days, freeze up to 1 month. Thaw at room temp or microwave 30 sec.
