## 1:10 PM — Roasted Vegetables (~1 hr total cook time, hands-on prep ~15 min)

**Ingredients:**
- 1 small butternut squash (quarter or 1/8 it)
- 1 small honey nut squash (halve it)
- 1 small delicata squash (slice into rings, seeds out)
- 1 small spaghetti squash (halve lengthwise, seeds out)
- 1 medium eggplant (1" cubes or batons)
- 3 tbsp ghee or olive oil
- Sea salt, black pepper, 1 tsp dried thyme
- Optional: ½ tsp garlic powder, ¼ tsp cumin

**Instructions:**
1. Prep veg: peel butternut (tough!) into 1–2" cubes; leave skin on honey nut + delicata for easier roasting. Scoop spaghetti squash seeds, leave halves whole. Cube eggplant.
2. Toss all veg on 2 sheet pans with ghee/oil, salt, pepper, thyme, optional spices. Spread in single layer.
3. Roast at 400°F (or 425°F convection) for 50–60 min, stirring pan halfway (25–30 min mark).
4. Veg is done when fork-tender and edges are golden. Cool on pans.
5. Once cooled, portion into airtight containers:
   - **Container 1:** Butternut + honey nut cubes (Monday–Wednesday roasted veg base)
   - **Container 2:** Delicata slices (Wednesday–Thursday side)
   - **Container 3:** Spaghetti squash halves (Tue D assembly + backup)
   - **Container 4:** Roasted eggplant (Tue D side + lunch rotation)
6. Label, date, refrigerate.

**Yield:** ~7 cups total roasted veg.

## 2:00 PM — Pan-Seared Paneer (while grains cool, ~10 min)

**Ingredients:**
- 8 oz paneer (from fridge)
- 1 tbsp ghee
- Sea salt, black pepper
- Fresh cilantro (optional garden herb)

**Instructions:**
1. Pat paneer dry with paper towel.
2. Cut into 1" cubes or 1/2" slices.
3. Heat ghee in cast-iron or nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
4. Working in batches (don't crowd), sear paneer 2–3 min per side until golden + crispy edges.
5. Cool on plate. Sprinkle cilantro if using.
6. Store in airtight container, fridge. Use by Thursday.

**Yield:** ~4 servings (2 oz per serving as lunch basket protein).

## 2:15 PM — Greens Prep (~10 min)

**Ingredients:**
- 3–4 bags fresh greens (romaine, mixed, spinach)
- Access to clean kitchen towel or salad spinner

**Instructions:**
1. Wash greens if not pre-washed.
2. Dry thoroughly (wet greens = faster wilting). Use spinner or paper towels.
3. Chop into bite-sized pieces if whole leaves are large.
4. Store in sealed container with paper towel lining (absorbs excess moisture).
5. Refrigerate. Good for 4–5 days; use fresh for sautéing Mon–Sat dinners.

**Yield:** ~8 cups washed, chopped greens (enough daily sauté + lunch baskets).

## 4:00 PM — Dinner Prep for Tonight (Sun D)

**Sun dinner: Black cod + roasted delicata + sautéed greens**

**Ingredients:**
- 1 black cod fillet (~6–8 oz, thawed Sun morning)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Sea salt, pepper
- ½ lemon, juiced + zest
- Fresh dill (from ahi package or garden)
- 1 delicata squash half (roasted, from prep above)
- 2 cups fresh greens (wash + chop from prep)
- 1 tbsp ghee (for sauté)
- 1 garlic clove, minced

**Instructions (cook at 5:00 PM):**
1. Heat skillet over medium-high. Sear black cod 4–5 min skin-side down, flip, cook 2–3 min until opaque (145°F). Top with dill + lemon.
2. Sauté greens in ghee + garlic 2–3 min until wilted. Season salt/pepper.
3. Reheat delicata squash in oven (350°F, 5–10 min) or microwave.
4. Plate: fish + squash + greens.

**Serving:** Both T + K enjoy. Leftovers: none expected (eat all).
