### Spinach Feta Cottage Cheese Egg Bake (Sat + Sun)
**Batch Sun for next week too · 45 min bake · 4 servings · 330 cal · 27g P**
- 8 eggs · ¾ cup cottage cheese (blended smooth)
- 4 cups baby spinach, wilted & squeezed
- ½ cup crumbled feta · 2 sliced green onions

### 🍛 Coconut Curry Chicken Bowl w/ Zucchini (Thu + Fri) — HR kit + chicken swap
**13 min · 2 servings · 480 cal · 40g P**
- HR: Coconut Curry Sauce pouch · Fresh Zucchini (1 medium)
- 8-10 oz pre-shredded chicken leg meat (freezer)
- 1 tbsp olive oil · ¾ cup pre-cooked quinoa per bowl · salt + pepper

## 🍽️ DINNERS

### 🥩 Ribeye + Chimichurri + Sweet Potato + Asparagus (Thu)
**25 min (+20m ribeye temper) · 550 cal · 42g P**
- 6-8 oz ribeye (thaw Wed night) · ½ sweet potato diced ½" · ½ bunch asparagus
- 1 tbsp avocado oil · salt · pepper · garlic powder
- Chimichurri: ¼ cup parsley · 2 tbsp cilantro · 1 garlic · 2 tbsp olive oil · 1 tbsp red wine vinegar · red pepper flakes · salt

1. 400°F. Roast veg w/ olive oil + salt 20 min.
2. Pat ribeye VERY dry. Season salt + pepper + garlic.
3. Cast iron on HIGH until smoking. Add avocado oil.
4. Sear ribeye 3 min per side to medium-rare (135°F internal).
5. REST 5 min. Slice against grain. Top w/ chimichurri.

### 🥩 Beef Chili + Avocado (Fri — batch 6 servings)
**25 min · 470 cal · 38g P per serving**
- 1 tbsp olive oil · 1 onion + 1 bell pepper diced · 3 garlic cloves · 1 lb ground beef
- 2 tbsp chili powder · 1 tbsp cumin · 1 tsp paprika · ½ tsp cinnamon · 1 tbsp tomato paste
- Cooked black beans (pantry — 1½ cups) · 1 can kidney beans · 1 jar red pasta sauce · 1 cup broth
- Toppings: avocado · Greek yogurt · cilantro

1. Sauté onion + pepper 5 min. Add garlic 30 sec.
2. Brown beef 5 min. Add spices + paste 1 min.
3. Add beans + sauce + broth. Simmer 20 min.
4. Top with avocado + yogurt + cilantro.

### 🍗 Za'atar Chicken + Broccoli + Goat Cheese + Quinoa (Sat)
**20 min · 490 cal · 44g P**
- 6-8 oz pre-shredded chicken leg meat · 1 tbsp za'atar · 1 tsp olive oil
- 2 cups broccoli florets · ¾ cup pre-cooked quinoa · 1 oz crumbled goat cheese · lemon

1. Toss chicken w/ za'atar + oil.
2. Roast broccoli at 425°F 12 min.
3. Pan-warm chicken 3 min.
4. Plate over quinoa, top w/ goat cheese crumble. Squeeze lemon.

### 🍗 Herb Roasted Chicken Thighs + Brussels + Sweet Potato (Sun — batch)
**45 min hands-off · 4 servings · 485 cal · 42g P**
- 4 bone-in skin-on chicken thighs · 1.5 lbs Brussels halved · 2 sweet potatoes diced
- 3 tbsp olive oil · 1 tsp za'atar + garlic powder + salt + pepper · 1 lemon

1. 425°F. Toss veg w/ 2 tbsp oil + salt.
2. Rub chicken w/ oil + spices.
3. Roast 35-40 min until 175°F. Lemon squeeze.
