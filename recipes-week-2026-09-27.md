## 🥩 Korean Beef Cube & Shiitake Stir-fry (Sun D, 3 serv)
**Time:** 20 min · **Macros (per T serv):** 520 cal · 42g P · 44g C · 18g F · 5g fib
**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb defrosted beef cubes, cut to 1" pieces
- 4 oz shiitake mushrooms, sliced
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 3 green onions, chopped
- 2" ginger, grated
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tbsp tamari · 1 tbsp sesame oil · 1 tbsp honey · 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 2 cups cooked rice
- Cilantro to finish, sesame seeds, kimchi (opt for K)

**Steps:**
1. Marinate cubes 30 min: tamari + sesame oil + ginger + garlic + honey + rice vinegar
2. Cast iron or wok HOT. Sear cubes 2 min per side, don't crowd. Remove.
3. Add shiitake + bell pepper, 4 min high heat.
4. Return beef + splash marinade + splash tamari, 2 min.
5. Serve over rice. Top green onion + sesame + cilantro. K: + kimchi.

**Note:** 🥩 Uses defrosted cubes + shiitake ⭐ + bell pepper. K's request tonight. NOT stew.

## 🧀 Palak Paneer (Tue D, 2 serv)
**Time:** 25 min · **Macros:** 460 cal · 28g P · 40g C · 22g F · 6g fib
**Ingredients:**
- 6 oz paneer, cubed
- Handful spinach (last of fresh)
- 1 onion diced · 3 garlic · 1.5" ginger grated
- 1 tsp cumin · 1 tsp coriander · 1 tsp turmeric · ½ tsp garam masala
- 1 roma tomato OR ½ can diced tomato
- ¼ cup cream OR ½ cup Greek yogurt (thick)
- Juice ½ lemon · cilantro · salt
- Basmati rice to serve

**Steps:**
1. Sear paneer cubes in ghee/oil 2 min per side, remove.
2. Sauté onion 5 min. Bloom garlic+ginger+spices 1 min.
3. Add tomato, 3 min till broken down.
4. Wilt spinach, 2 min.
5. Blend (or leave chunky). Return to pot, stir in yogurt/cream low heat (don't boil).
6. Add paneer back, warm 3 min. Finish lemon + cilantro.
7. Serve over basmati.

**Note:** 🧀 Uses paneer ⭐ + last handful spinach. Comfort food solo night. Yogurt cheaper than cream + higher protein.

## 🌶️ Ground Beef Kofta + Yogurt Sauce (Fri D, 8 patties → 4 tonight + 4 freezer raw)
**Time:** 20 min · **Macros:** 500 cal · 40g P · 30g C · 22g F · 6g fib
**Ingredients (kofta):**
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 small onion grated (or ½ cup)
- 3 garlic minced
- 1 tsp cumin · 1 tsp coriander · ½ tsp cinnamon · 1 tsp paprika · salt/pepper
- ¼ cup cilantro + parsley chopped
- 1 tbsp olive oil (cook)

**Yogurt sauce:** ½ cup Greek yogurt · ½ cucumber grated (squeeze water out) · 1 garlic minced · dill · lemon · salt

**Sides:** Rice + green salad + lemon-tahini

**Steps:**
1. Mix kofta ingredients. Form 8 oval patties. Freeze 4 raw between parchment.
2. Cook 4 in skillet olive oil, 4 min per side.
3. Yogurt sauce: mix all, chill.
4. Plate: kofta + rice + salad + yogurt sauce.

**Note:** 🌶️ Uses 1 lb of 5 lb ground beef stash. 4 raw kofta banked for future weeks.
