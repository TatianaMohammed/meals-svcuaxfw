# Meal plan — November 1–7, 2026 · 🫙 Glass-Dish Bake Week

**Theme:** Assemble ahead in glass dishes, then bake. TikTok-style baked protein pancakes for breakfast, plus casserole and fish bakes made from the freezer and the sauces already in the pantry.

**Household:** Both home Sun · **K away Mon → back Wed night** (no travel food) · Both home Thu–Sat (⚠️ confirm K is home Sat — if not, his Sat portions go to the freezer).

**Targets:** Tatiana ~1,450 kcal · 110–130g P · ~30g fiber | Khalid ~1,800 kcal · ~130g P · 1 protein bar/day when home.

---

## Strategy

- **Sunday bake-off:** 2 protein pancake bakes (8×8), Birria-sauce beef + red lentil casserole (9×13), roasted rutabaga + Brussels, quinoa, hard-boiled eggs, shredded rotisserie chicken.
- **Fish while T is solo (Mon–Wed):** Chinook salmon Mon, trout Tue. Each bake makes 2 portions, so tonight's dinner is also tomorrow's lunch.
- **Wed:** Thai peanut chicken rice-ramen bake (assembled Tue night). Bake 2 portions: one for T, one boxed for K when he's back.
- **Thu–Sat (both):** Lamb chop sheet pan, then a GF pasta bake (assembled Thu night), then ribeye night.
- **Costco rotisserie chicken (K picks up Sat/Sun AM)** covers Sun lunch and the Wed bake. Use it by Wed.
- ⚠️ **Glass safety:** never move a glass dish straight from the fridge into a hot oven. Let it sit out 15–20 minutes, or put it in while the oven preheats. Take snap lids off before baking.

Check the labels on **birria sauce, Thai peanut sauce, red pepper almond pesto, cilantro-lime sauce, rice ramen, and the rotisserie chicken**. **Hoisin, black pepper sauce and miso are NOT used this week** because they often contain wheat. Check them before any future use.
