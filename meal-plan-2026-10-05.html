Banana muffins — no blueberries (12 muffins)

About 15 minutes prep + 18–22 minutes baking; check earlier because batter volume is smaller

  • ¾ cup almond flour
  • ¾ cup certified gluten-free oat flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda; 1 tsp baking powder
  • ¾ tsp cinnamon; ½ tsp salt; ¼ tsp ginger
  • 2 medium mashed bananas (about 1 cup)
  • ¼ cup honey
  • ⅓ cup cottage cheese
  • ¼ cup smooth natural peanut butter
  • ¼ cup reduced-fat milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 1½ tsp vanilla; cooking spray
  1. Heat oven to 350°F and grease a 12-cup muffin tin.
  2. Whisk the flours, leavening, cinnamon, salt and ginger.
  3. Beat banana, honey, cottage cheese, peanut butter, milk, egg and vanilla until combined.
  4. Mix in dry ingredients just until combined. Omit blueberries; do not add a replacement.
  5. Divide all batter evenly among 12 cups, rather than insisting on the original ⅓-cup fill.
  6. Bake until set and a tester comes out clean. Start checking early; original timing is 18–22 minutes.
  7. Cool 5 minutes in the pan, then on a rack. Freeze extra muffins.
