### Spinach Feta Cottage Cheese Egg Bake (Sunday batch → Wed + Sat)
**45 min bake · 4 servings · 330 cal · 27g P**
- 8 eggs · ¾ cup cottage cheese (**blended smooth** — no curds visible)
- 4 cups baby spinach, wilted & squeezed

### 🍛 Weeknight Chicken Biryani + Cucumber Raita (Fri) ⭐ NEW
**35 min · 2 servings · 495 cal · 42g P**

**Chicken layer:**
- 8-10 oz pulled chicken leg meat (mom's)
- ½ cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tsp each: ginger paste, garlic paste
- 1 tsp garam masala · ½ tsp turmeric · ½ tsp cumin · ½ tsp coriander · pinch cayenne
- Salt · 2 tbsp cilantro + mint chopped

**Rice layer:**
- 1 cup basmati rice, rinsed until clear
- 1½ cups water
- 1 bay leaf, 2 cardamom pods, 1 cinnamon stick, 3 cloves
- ½ tsp salt · pinch saffron in 2 tbsp warm water

**Aromatics:** 1 tbsp avocado oil · 1 large yellow onion sliced thin

1. **Rice:** Rinse basmati 3x. Combine with water, whole spices, salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 15 min. Rest covered 5 min. Fluff.
2. **Crispy onions:** Heat avocado oil in wide skillet. Cook sliced onion 8-10 min stirring, until deeply golden with crispy edges. Reserve ⅔ for garnish; leave ⅓ in pan.
3. **Chicken:** Mix pulled chicken with yogurt + all spices + salt. Add to pan with onions. Cook 5 min medium — warm through, thicken slightly. Fold in cilantro + mint.
4. **Assemble:** Spread ⅓ rice in pan, all chicken on top, remaining rice. Drizzle saffron water over top. Cover, lowest heat 5 min (short "dum").
5. **Serve:** Fluff gently. Top with reserved crispy onions, cilantro, lemon squeeze.

**Raita:** ¼ cup Greek yogurt + ¼ diced cucumber + cumin + salt + mint.
