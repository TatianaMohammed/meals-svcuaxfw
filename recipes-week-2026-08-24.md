## 🥫 BATCH DRESSINGS (Sunday prep — ½ cup mason jar each)

### Lemon Vinaigrette
- ¼ cup olive oil
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice (~1 lemon)
- 1 tsp Dijon (or 1 tsp miso paste for umami)
- 1 tsp honey or maple
- 1 small garlic clove, grated
- Salt + pepper
- **1 tbsp chopped fresh thyme** (garden)

Shake in mason jar. Keeps 1 wk.

### Miso-Tahini Dressing
- 3 tbsp tahini
- 2 tbsp miso paste
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar (or lemon juice)
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp tamari or coconut aminos
- 1 tbsp maple or honey
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 3–4 tbsp warm water to thin

Whisk in mason jar. Keeps 1 wk. Uses: chicken bowls, salmon rice bowl, veg dips.

## 🐟 MON — Roasted Salmon Sheet Pan (batch 4)

**Feeds Mon D both + Tue L both. 40 min total, 10 min hands-on.**

- 4 salmon portions (~6 oz each)
- 2 medium sweet potatoes, ½" cubes
- 1 head broccoli, florets
- 1 zucchini, sliced ½" thick (fridge rescue)
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper
- **Fresh thyme + marjoram** (garden — 1 tbsp each, chopped)
- 1 lemon, sliced
- **Fresh cilantro** to finish (fridge rescue — chop ¼ cup)

1. 425°F. Toss sweet potato + oil + salt on sheet pan → roast 15 min.
2. Add broccoli + zucchini + more oil. Push to sides, add salmon in middle.
3. Top salmon with thyme + marjoram + lemon slices + salt. Roast 12–14 min.
4. Finish with cilantro. Serve 2 with rice on the side (Khalid), save 2 salmon portions + rice for Tue L bowls.

## 🐟 WED — Coconut Curry Baked Cod (2 portions)

**25 min total, 10 min hands-on.**

- 2 cod portions (~6 oz each)
- 2 coconut curry sauce cubes (freezer — thaw in fridge Tue night)
- 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed
- 1 cup quinoa (pre-cooked Sun prep)
- Olive oil, salt, pepper
- **Fresh basil + marjoram** (garden — chopped)
- Lemon wedges

1. 400°F. Toss asparagus with oil + salt on sheet pan → roast 8 min.
2. Push asparagus to side. Add cod, salt lightly.
3. Melt coconut curry cubes 30 sec in microwave, pour over cod.
4. Roast 10–12 min until cod flakes.
5. Warm quinoa, toss with chopped basil.
6. Plate: quinoa base, asparagus, cod with sauce. Top cod with basil + marjoram, lemon on side. Khalid gets extra quinoa + ½ avocado.

## 🫘 SAT — Red Lentil Dal (pantry — batch 3)

**Feeds Sat D + Sun L + 1 spare. 30 min.**

- 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
- 3 cups water or chicken stock (freezer)
- 1 small onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp ginger, grated
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp coriander
- ½ tsp cayenne (optional)
- 1 tbsp ghee or coconut oil
- Salt to taste
- Basmati rice on the side
- **Fresh basil** to finish

1. Sauté onion + garlic + ginger + spices in ghee, 3 min.
2. Add lentils + stock, simmer 20 min until soft.
3. Salt to taste. Top with soft egg and basil ribbons at serving.

## 🍗 FRI / SUN — Roasted Chicken Legs (batch 6, from freezer)

**Fri = feeds both + Sat shreds. Sun = feeds Tatiana + shreds + freezer restock.**

- 6 frozen chicken legs (thaw in fridge 24h ahead)
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- **Fresh thyme + marjoram** (garden — 2 tbsp each, chopped)
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder
- 2 sweet potatoes, cubed
- 1 lb Brussels sprouts, halved (Fri) / mixed greens salad (Sun)

1. 425°F. Rub chicken with oil + herbs + salt + pepper + garlic powder.
2. Sheet pan: chicken + sweet potato + Brussels (Fri only).
3. Roast 40 min until skin crisp and internal 175°F.
4. **Fri**: eat 2 legs each, shred 2 into container for Sat L.
5. **Sun**: eat 2, shred 2 for next week, freeze 2 whole cooked legs.
