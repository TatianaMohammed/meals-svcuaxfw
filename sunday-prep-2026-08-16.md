## ⏰ Timed schedule

### 12:00 — Kick off long-cooks
- [ ] **Soak 1 cup red lentils** in bowl of water (for lentil bread — needs 2–4h)
- [ ] **Start chicken stock**: dump frozen veg scraps into stockpot + 8 cups water + 1 tsp salt + 1 bay leaf + peppercorns. If you have any chicken bones/carcasses in freezer, add those. Bring to boil, then simmer covered 3 hours.
- [ ] **Pull 6 chicken legs from freezer** into fridge to thaw for tomorrow's shredding (or if pre-cooked/grilled by mom, they can go directly to shredding step at 3pm)

### 12:30 — Groceries + errands
- [ ] Costco/TJs run (see grocery-week-2026-08-17.md)
- [ ] **Buy fresh salmon (2 portions)** for Mon dinner

### 3:00 — Batch cooking wave
- [ ] **Cook 1 cup quinoa** in rice cooker or pot (2 cups water) — 15 min → portion into 4 x ¾-cup containers
- [ ] **Blend lentil bread batter**: drained soaked lentils + 2 eggs + 3 tbsp olive oil + 1 tsp baking powder + 1 tsp salt + optional herbs → blend smooth. Pour into greased loaf pan.
- [ ] **Bake lentil bread** 350°F for 40 min
- [ ] **Hard-boil 6 eggs** (12 min from cold water start, ice bath after)

### 4:00 — Egg bake + Parm crisps
- [ ] **Bake Spinach Feta CC egg bake** (see recipe) — 30–35 min at 375°F → cut into 4 squares
- [ ] **Bake 12 Parm crisps** in same oven: 1 tbsp Parm mounds on parchment · 350°F 5–7 min → cool, store in airtight

### 5:00 — Shred + portion
- [ ] **Shred chicken legs** — pull ~14 oz meat, portion into 2 labeled containers (Wed L: 4 oz · Sat L: 6 oz · leftover 4 oz for weekend flex)
- [ ] **Strain chicken stock** through fine mesh — cool, portion into 2-cup mason jars (fridge 3, freeze rest)
- [ ] **Wash + portion berries** into snack-size containers

### 5:30 — Dressings + finishing
- [ ] **Lemon Vinaigrette** (mason jar ½ cup): juice of 1 lemon + ¼ cup olive oil + 1 tsp Dijon + 1 tsp honey + salt/pep. Shake.
- [ ] **Miso-Tahini Dressing** (mason jar ½ cup): 2 tbsp tahini + 1 tbsp white miso + 2 tbsp lemon juice + 2 tbsp warm water + 1 tsp honey + garlic. Whisk.
- [ ] Pull lentil bread from oven → cool 20 min → slice → wrap in foil (fridge)
- [ ] Pull egg bake → cool → cut into 4 squares → containers

### 6:00 — Wed pm bonus prep (10 sec — do it Wed evening, not today)
- [ ] Assemble Thu overnight oats jar
