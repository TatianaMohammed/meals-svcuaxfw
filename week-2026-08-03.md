## Gluten-free notes to watch
- **Soy sauce → tamari** (many soy sauces contain wheat)
- **Oats → certified GF oats** (regular oats are cross-contaminated)
- **Bread → Canyon Bakehouse or Little Northern Bakehouse** (best GF sandwich breads)
- **Chicken sausage → check label** for gluten-free (Aidells is GF, most Applegate too)
- **Marinara → Rao's** (naturally GF, no thickeners)
- **Chili powder → check label** for cross-contamination (McCormick GF)
