## 🍳 BREAKFASTS

### `overnight-oats` — Berry Almond Overnight Oats
**Prep:** 5 min night before | **Makes:** 1 serving (batch 3 jars on Sunday)

**Ingredients**
- ½ cup old-fashioned rolled oats
- ¾ cup unsweetened almond milk
- ½ cup plain 2% Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
- ½ cup mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
- 1 tbsp chopped walnuts (add in morning to stay crunchy)
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- Splash of vanilla

**Instructions**
1. Combine everything except walnuts in a 16 oz mason jar.
2. Stir well, cap, refrigerate overnight (or up to 4 days).
3. Morning: top with walnuts and eat cold or warm 60 sec in microwave.

**Macros:** 340 cal | 22g P | 42g C | 10g F | 8g fiber

### `egg-scramble` — Spinach Feta Egg Scramble + Toast
**Prep:** 8 min | **Makes:** 1 serving

**Ingredients**
- 2 whole eggs + 1 egg white
- 1 cup baby spinach
- 1 tbsp crumbled feta
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1 slice Ezekiel or sprouted whole-grain toast
- Salt, pepper, pinch of turmeric

**Instructions**
1. Heat olive oil in nonstick pan over medium. Add spinach, wilt 30 sec.
2. Whisk eggs + white with turmeric, salt, pepper. Pour over spinach.
3. Scramble gently, sprinkle feta in last 30 sec.
4. Serve with toast (dry or with ½ tsp butter).

**Macros:** 330 cal | 25g P | 22g C | 15g F | 4g fiber

### `lentil-soup` — Lentil Soup w/ Chicken Sausage & Greens
**Prep:** 30 min | **Makes:** 4 servings (batch on Sunday)

**Ingredients**
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 carrots, diced (optional; skip if not on list)
- 2 links chicken sausage, sliced
- 1 cup dry green or brown lentils, rinsed
- 1 can (28 oz) diced tomatoes, no salt added
- 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp smoked paprika
- 2 cups chopped kale or Swiss chard
- Salt, pepper, lemon wedges

**Instructions**
1. Heat oil in Dutch oven. Sauté onion 4 min; add garlic + sausage, cook 3 min.
2. Add lentils, tomatoes, broth, spices. Bring to boil, reduce, simmer 25 min until lentils tender.
3. Stir in kale, cook 3 min. Season, finish with lemon squeeze.
4. Portion into 4 containers. Keeps 5 days refrigerated; freezes 3 months.

**Macros per serving:** 435 cal | 32g P | 42g C | 12g F | 12g fiber

## 🍽️ DINNERS

### `sheet-pan-salmon` — Sheet Pan Salmon + Asparagus + Sweet Potato
**Prep:** 25 min total | **Makes:** 2 servings (one for dinner, one for Wed lunch)

**Ingredients**
- 2 salmon fillets (5 oz each), skin on
- 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed
- 1 medium sweet potato, diced ½"
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp turmeric, salt, pepper
- 1 lemon, sliced

**Instructions**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss sweet potato with 1 tbsp oil + salt on sheet pan. Roast 12 min.
2. Push potato to one side. Add asparagus (drizzled with oil) and salmon (rubbed with oil + spices, topped with lemon).
3. Roast 12 more minutes until salmon flakes and asparagus is tender-crisp.

**Macros per serving:** 470 cal | 36g P | 32g C | 20g F | 7g fiber

### `turkey-meatballs` — Turkey Meatballs + Zoodles + Marinara
**Prep:** 30 min | **Makes:** 4 servings (2 dinners + 2 freezer)

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb ground turkey (93/7)
- 1 egg
- ⅓ cup grated Parmesan (or omit)
- 2 tbsp almond flour (or oat flour)
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning, salt, pepper
- 3 zucchini, spiralized (or 1 pack pre-spiralized)
- 1 jar Rao's marinara (low sugar)

**Instructions**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Mix turkey, egg, Parm, almond flour, garlic, parsley, seasonings. Form 12 meatballs.
2. Bake on parchment-lined sheet 18–20 min until 165°F internal.
3. Meanwhile, sauté zoodles in dry pan 3 min (drain excess water). Warm marinara.
4. Serve 3 meatballs over zoodles with ½ cup sauce. Freeze remaining meatballs cooked.

**Macros per serving (3 meatballs + zoodles + sauce):** 435 cal | 38g P | 22g C | 20g F | 6g fiber

### `turkey-chili` — Turkey Chili
**Prep:** 40 min | **Makes:** 6 servings (freezer-friendly)

**Ingredients**
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 lb ground turkey (93/7)
- 2 tbsp chili powder, 1 tbsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp cinnamon
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 can (28 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 can black beans, rinsed
- 1 can kidney beans, rinsed
- 1 cup chicken broth
- Salt, pepper
- **Toppings:** ¼ avocado, 2 tbsp Greek yogurt, fresh cilantro

**Instructions**
1. Heat oil in Dutch oven. Sauté onion + pepper 5 min. Add garlic 30 sec.
2. Add turkey, brown 5 min. Add spices + tomato paste, stir 1 min.
3. Add tomatoes, beans, broth. Simmer 25 min uncovered.
4. Portion into 6 containers. Fridge 4 days; freeze 3 months.

**Macros per serving (with toppings):** 465 cal | 38g P | 42g C | 15g F | 14g fiber

### `roasted-chicken-thighs` — Herb Roasted Chicken Thighs + Brussels + Sweet Potato
**Prep:** 45 min | **Makes:** 4 servings (dinner + 3 lunch bases)

**Ingredients**
- 4 bone-in skin-on chicken thighs
- 1.5 lbs Brussels sprouts, halved
- 2 sweet potatoes, diced
- 3 tbsp olive oil (divided)
- 1 tsp za'atar or Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, pepper
- 1 lemon

**Instructions**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss Brussels + sweet potato with 2 tbsp oil, salt, pepper on 2 sheet pans.
2. Pat chicken dry; rub with 1 tbsp oil + spices. Place on one pan with veg.
3. Roast 35–40 min until chicken hits 175°F internal and skin is crispy.
4. Squeeze lemon over everything.

**Macros per serving:** 485 cal | 42g P | 35g C | 18g F | 8g fiber

*Extra: shred remaining chicken meat for Monday's Mediterranean bowl.*
