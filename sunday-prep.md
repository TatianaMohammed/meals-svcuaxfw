# Sunday 90-Minute Meal Prep

Do this Sunday afternoon and 80% of your week is already assembled.

## Timeline (parallel workflow)

### 0:00 — Preheat & start slow items
- [ ] Preheat oven to 425°F
- [ ] Start 1.5 cups dry quinoa in rice cooker or pot (2 min → walk away for 15)
- [ ] Fill pot for 8 hard-boiled eggs, bring to boil

### 0:10 — Sheet pans in oven
- [ ] **Sheet pan 1:** 2 diced sweet potatoes + 2 bell peppers + 1 broccoli head, tossed in 2 tbsp olive oil + salt
- [ ] **Sheet pan 2:** 2 lbs chicken breasts, rubbed with olive oil + garlic + oregano + paprika
- [ ] Both go in oven; chicken 22–25 min, veg 25–30 min

### 0:15 — Overnight oats (batch 3 jars)
- [ ] In 3 mason jars, combine per jar: ½ cup oats, ¾ cup almond milk, ½ cup Greek yogurt, 1 tbsp chia, 1 tbsp flax, cinnamon, splash vanilla
- [ ] Cap, refrigerate. (Add berries + walnuts each morning.)

### 0:25 — Eggs done
- [ ] Turn off heat when boiling, cover, set timer 10 min → transfer to ice bath → peel and refrigerate in a covered container

### 0:35 — Turkey chili (or Sunday dinner chicken thighs)
- [ ] Pick ONE for tonight based on what sounds good:
  - **Option A:** Start turkey chili → 40 min simmer (see recipe)
  - **Option B:** Start herb roasted chicken thighs → 40 min bake

### 0:45 — Chop & wash produce
- [ ] Wash all berries (dry thoroughly with paper towels — wet berries mold fast)
- [ ] Dice 2 cucumbers, halve 2 pints cherry tomatoes → store in containers
- [ ] Cut bell peppers into snack strips
- [ ] Portion 3 tbsp hummus into 3 small containers with veggie sticks

### 1:00 — Ovens out
- [ ] Cool veg 10 min, pack into container (5-day fridge life)
- [ ] Slice 4 oz chicken portions into 4 containers (Mon–Thu lunches)
- [ ] Quinoa done → portion 4 × ¾ cup into containers

### 1:15 — Assemble Monday lunch (Mediterranean bowl)
- [ ] Layer 1 mason jar with dressing at bottom, then quinoa, chicken, chickpeas, veggies, feta on top → grab-and-go tomorrow

### 1:30 — DONE ✅

## Pro tips for Wegovy
- **Portion small.** With reduced appetite, half a serving may fill you. Store the rest for tomorrow — never force it.
- **Sip liquids AWAY from meals** (30 min before/after) to reduce nausea and let food fit.
- **Protein first, always.** Eat the protein portion of your meal first before carbs/fats.
- **Cold foods = easier when nauseous.** Chia pudding, overnight oats, salmon salad are your friends on rough days.
- **Ginger tea** in the pantry — anti-inflammatory AND settles Wegovy nausea.
