### 🎯 Creamy Scallops Truffle Linguine w/ Shiitakes + Spinach (Sun L, 2 serv, ~460 cal / 34g P)
**Ingredients (2 serv):**
- 1 pkg HR Cauliflower Linguine
- HR Wild-Caught Scallops (split 2 servings)
- 1 cup HR Creamy Rosemary Mushroom Truffle Sauce
- 4 shiitake mushrooms, sliced
- 2 cups spinach
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp parm + fresh thyme + s/p + olive oil

**Method:**
1. Boil cauliflower linguine per pkg. Reserve ¼ cup pasta water.
2. Pat scallops dry, season w/ salt. Sear in hot skillet w/ 1 tbsp oil, 2 min/side until golden. Remove.
3. Same pan: shiitakes + garlic in bit more oil, 3 min until softened.
4. Add truffle sauce + splash pasta water, warm through.
5. Toss in linguine + spinach. Wilt.
6. Plate, top w/ scallops + parm + thyme.

**Note:** TTC-safe. Scallops = bivalves (low mercury, fine to eat). Shiitakes + truffle = double umami.

### 🥩 Herb-Butter Ribeye + Roasted Eggplant + Creamy Polenta (Sun D, 2 serv, ~550 cal / 42g P)
**Ingredients (2 serv):**
- 1 ribeye (14-16 oz) — split T + K
- 2 tbsp garlic-herb butter
- 1 eggplant, ½" rounds (part of Sun batch)
- 2 servings polenta (Sun batch)
- Fresh thyme + marjoram + basil, s/p

**Method:**
1. Rest ribeye 30 min at room temp. Season heavily w/ salt + pepper.
2. Hot cast iron, 1 tbsp oil, sear 4 min side 1, 3 min side 2 for med-rare.
3. Off heat: dollop garlic-herb butter + thyme + marjoram. Baste. Rest 8 min.
4. Slice against grain.
5. Plate over polenta (add splash milk + parm to reheat), fan roasted eggplant, top w/ basil.

**Note:** Save 3 ribeyes for later. Herb butter carries the whole plate.

### 🐟 Miso-Ginger Cod + Roasted Eggplant + Quinoa (Mon D, 2 serv, ~470 cal / 40g P)
**Ingredients (2 serv):**
- 2 thawed cod portions (~6 oz each)
- 2 tbsp brown miso paste
- 1 tsp ginger paste
- 1 tbsp tamari + 1 tsp sesame oil + 1 tsp honey
- 1 eggplant roasted rounds (from Sun batch)
- 1 cup cooked quinoa
- Green onion + sesame seeds

**Method:**
1. Whisk miso + ginger + tamari + sesame + honey.
2. Pat cod dry, brush w/ glaze. Broil 8 min or bake 400°F 12 min until flakes.
3. Reheat eggplant, drizzle sesame + green onion.
4. Serve over quinoa, top cod w/ remaining glaze + sesame seeds.

**Note:** TTC power dinner. Cod = low-mercury DHA. Uses brown miso pantry item + ginger paste.

### 🍲 Beef Cube Stew w/ Cream of Celery, Shiitakes & Rosemary — over Polenta (Wed D, 2 serv, ~490 cal / 40g P)
**Ingredients (2 serv):**
- 1 lb beef cubes
- 1 can cream of celery
- 1 cup chicken stock (from Sun poach)
- 2 carrots, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic
- Any remaining shiitakes (or button mushrooms), sliced
- 1 tbsp fresh rosemary + 1 tsp thyme + 1 tsp chopped lovage
- 2 tbsp olive oil, s/p
- 2 servings polenta (from Sun batch)

**Method:**
1. Salt beef cubes. Sear in oil in Dutch oven, 4 min per side. Remove.
2. Same pot: onion + garlic + carrot + mushrooms, 6 min.
3. Return beef. Add cream of celery + stock + rosemary + thyme. Simmer covered 45–60 min until tender.
4. Stir in lovage last minute.
5. Reheat polenta w/ splash milk + parm. Ladle stew over.

**Note:** Anti-inflam TTC-friendly. Uses cream of celery + lovage + rosemary garden herbs. Polenta 2nd use.

### Skillet Eggplant Parmesan (Sat D, 2 serv + Sun L leftover option, ~500 cal / 28g P)
**Ingredients:**
- 2 eggplants, sliced ½" rounds (raw)
- 1 jar marinara
- ½ cup sun-dried tomatoes (chopped)
- ½ cup grated parm + mozz if have
- Fresh basil + thyme + marjoram
- Olive oil, s/p
- Optional side: GF pasta

**Method:**
1. Salt eggplant rounds 15 min, pat dry.
2. Pan-fry rounds in olive oil 3 min/side until golden. Set aside.
3. Same pan: marinara + sun-dried tomatoes, warm 3 min.
4. Layer in skillet: sauce → eggplant → parm → repeat. Top w/ herbs.
5. Broil 4 min until bubbly + browned.
6. Serve as-is or w/ GF pasta.

**Note:** Marinara jar #2 done (2 left). Sun-dried tomato feature. Finishes eggplants.
