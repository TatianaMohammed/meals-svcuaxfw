Weekly Meal Plan

Sunday Meal Prep

Every prep-ahead task called out explicitly — shredding, roasting, dressings, portioning.

Design Rules (every week)

  • 4'11" · ~195 lbs · GF · No shellfish · TTC-safe
  • 🐟 Fresh fish clustered Mon-Wed (2-day fridge life after thaw)
  • 🥗 Max 2 batch dressings per week — one citrus + one creamy
  • 🔪 All shredding, roasting, dressings, portioning done Sunday
  • 🥩 Beyond Meat, ground beef, cubes rotate through the week

TTC / Anti-inflammatory backbone

  • ✅ Wild salmon 2x/week (low-mercury, high DHA)
  • ✅ Eggs daily for choline
  • ✅ Dark leafy greens + lentils/beans for folate & iron
  • ✅ Full-fat dairy in moderation
  • ✅ Kiwi vitamin C boosts iron absorption from lentils/beef
  • ❌ No swordfish, king mackerel, tilefish, marlin, bigeye tuna
  • ❌ No raw fish, raw eggs, unpasteurized dairy, unheated deli meat
  • ⚠️ Caffeine ≤200 mg/day (~1 medium coffee)

Wegovy eating tips

  • Protein first, always
  • Sip liquids AWAY from meals (30 min before/after)
  • Portion small; save the rest
  • Cold foods on nauseous days
  • Ginger tea = anti-inflammatory + settles nausea
