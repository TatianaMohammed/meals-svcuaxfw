## 🥞 1. Blueberry-Banana Baked Protein Pancake (8×8) — Sun bake
**6 squares · ~180 cal · 18P · 22C · 5F · 3 fib each** · 10 min prep + 22–25 min bake

**Ingredients**
- 2 scoops Ritual Essential Protein
- 1 cup GF oats
- 1 cup liquid egg whites
- 2 whole eggs
- ¾ cup (6 oz) cottage cheese (uses up the open container)
- 1 ripe banana
- 1 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp vanilla, pinch salt
- ½ cup frozen blueberries (for the top)

**Instructions**
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease the 8×8 glass dish with ghee.
2. Blend everything except the blueberries until smooth, about 30 sec.
3. Pour into the dish and scatter the blueberries on top.
4. Bake 22–25 min, until the center is set and a toothpick comes out clean.
5. Cool 10 min, then cut into 6 squares.

## 🌮 4. Birria-Sauce Beef & Red Lentil Casserole (9×13) — Sun D
**8 servings · per serving ~330 cal · 25P · 22C · 15F · 4 fib**. With toppings + greens: **395 / 29P / 6 fib**. 20 min stovetop + 25 min bake.

**Ingredients**
- 1.5 lb ground beef
- 1 cup dry red lentils, rinsed (simmered 12 min in 2½ cups water until just soft, drained)
- 1 yellow onion, diced; 3 garlic cloves
- 1 cup birria cooking sauce (⚠️ GF label check)
- 4 cups spinach, chopped
- 2 tbsp fresh lovage, chopped (celery-like)
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella
- **Toppings per serving:** 2 tbsp Greek yogurt · 1 tbsp cilantro-lime sauce · lime wedge
- **Side:** 2 cups greens (romaine or spinach for K)

**Instructions**
1. Brown the beef in a skillet and drain the fat. Add the onion and garlic and cook 4 min.
2. Stir in the birria sauce, cooked lentils, spinach and lovage. Simmer 3 min.
3. Spread in a greased 9×13 and top with mozzarella.
4. Bake at 375°F for 20–25 min, until bubbling and golden.
5. **Portions:** Sun D (T 1, K 1.5), Thu L (T 1 + K 1.5 → **freeze tonight**), 3 spare → freeze.

## 🐟 5. Chinook Salmon Pesto Bake (2 single glass dishes) — Mon D + Tue L
**Per portion: 415 cal · 35P · 14C · 22F · 6 fib** · 5 min assembly + 15–18 min bake

**Ingredients (2 portions)**
- ~10 oz Chinook salmon (thawed), cut into 2 × 5 oz pieces
- 3 cups broccoli florets
- 4 tsp red pepper almond pesto (⚠️ GF check)
- 2 tbsp sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
- ½ cup cooked quinoa
- Lemon, salt, pepper

**Instructions**
1. Heat the oven to 400°F. In each glass dish, layer ¼ cup quinoa, then 1½ cups broccoli.
2. Set the salmon skin-side down on top. Spread 2 tsp pesto over each piece and add the sun-dried tomatoes.
3. Bake 15–18 min, until the salmon flakes and reads 145°F. Squeeze lemon over.
4. **Bones:** Chinook with bones lifts off cleanly once baked. Pull the pin bones and backbone before eating or packing.
5. Lid portion #2 once cool and refrigerate it for Tue lunch.

## 🐟 6. Lemon-Dill Trout Bake with Capers + Sun-Dried Tomato — Tue D + Wed L
**Per portion: 380 cal · 33P · 15C · 16F · 6 fib** · 5 min assembly + 12–15 min bake

**Ingredients (2 portions)**

- 2 cups roasted Brussels sprouts + 1½ cups roasted rutabaga (from Sun prep)
- 1 tbsp capers, 2 tbsp sun-dried tomatoes
- 2 tsp olive oil, 1 lemon (zest + slices)
- Fresh garden dill, salt, pepper

**Instructions**
1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Split the roasted Brussels + rutabaga between 2 glass dishes.
2. Lay the trout skin-side down on top. Brush with olive oil and season.
3. Add capers, sun-dried tomatoes, lemon zest and lemon slices.
4. Bake 12–15 min, until opaque and flaky (145°F).
5. Finish with lots of fresh dill. Portion #2 → Wed lunch.

## 🐑 8. Rosemary-Marjoram Lamb Chop Sheet Pan — Thu D (+ Fri L)
**T (2 chops + veg): 450 cal · 37P · 14C · 28F · 4 fib** · K 3 chops ~620 / 53P · 15 min prep + 25 min roast

**Ingredients**
- 9 lamb loin chops (thawed)
- 1 rutabaga (#2), peeled, ¾" cubes
- 3 cups broccoli florets
- 1 red onion, wedges
- 2 tbsp olive oil, 4 garlic cloves (grated)
- 1 tbsp fresh rosemary + 1 tbsp fresh marjoram (chopped), lemon zest, salt, pepper

**Instructions**
1. Rub the chops with 1 tbsp oil, garlic, rosemary, marjoram, zest, salt and pepper. Rest 20 min.
2. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the rutabaga with oil and salt, then roast **15 min first** (rutabaga takes longer).
3. Add the broccoli and onion, then nestle the chops on top. Roast 10–12 min to medium (135–140°F, rest to 145°F).
4. **Portions:** Thu D T 2, K 3. **Fri L:** T 2, K 2 → cool and refrigerate.

**8 servings · ~330 cal · 23P each** (T portion + side salad = 360 / 24P) · 20 min assembly + 35 min bake

- 2½ cups rustic pasta sauce
- 4 cups spinach
- **Protein layer:** 1 cup cottage cheese + 1 cup liquid egg whites + 1 tsp Italian herbs + pinch salt, whisked

**Instructions**

4. **Fri:** sit out 20 min. Bake covered at 375°F for 25 min, then uncovered 10–15 min until the center is hot and set (160°F, because of the egg layer).

6. **Portions:** Fri D T 1, K 2 · Sat L T 1, K 1.5 · freeze 2–3 single portions.

## 🍲 13. Golden Chicken, Rutabaga & Red Lentil Soup (freezer batch, low-histamine, anti-inflammatory)
**8 servings (~1¾ cups each) · per serving ~340 cal · 44P · 28C · 7F · 7 fib**
T = 1 serving (340 / 44P) · K = 1.5 servings (510 / 66P) · 15 min prep + 35 min simmer (or 12 min Instant Pot)

**Ingredients**
- 2 lb **fresh** boneless skinless chicken breast + 1 lb boneless thighs (bought fresh, cooked the same day)
- 1 cup dry red lentils, rinsed
- 1 rutabaga (#3), peeled, ¾" cubes
- 4 carrots, sliced · 3 celery stalks, sliced · 1 yellow onion, diced · 1 zucchini, diced
- 2 cups broccoli florets (stir in for the last 5 min)
- 4 garlic cloves · 2" fresh ginger, grated · 1½ tsp ground turmeric · pinch black pepper (helps turmeric absorb)
- 2 tbsp olive oil · 1½ tsp salt
- **10 cups water.** No store-bought broth or bone broth, both of which are high-histamine.
- Garden herbs: 6 sprigs thyme + 1 sprig rosemary (in the pot) · 2 tbsp lovage + basil (to finish)

**Instructions**
1. Warm the olive oil in a large pot. Cook the onion, carrot and celery 5 min, then add the garlic, ginger and turmeric for 1 min.
2. Add the water, lentils, rutabaga, whole chicken pieces, thyme, rosemary and salt. Bring to a boil, then simmer 25 min.
3. Pull out the chicken and shred it. Add the zucchini + broccoli and simmer 5 min. Return the chicken, remove the herb stems, and stir in the lovage and basil.
4. **Low-histamine cooling (important):** set the pot in a sink ice bath and stir until lukewarm, within about 30 min.
5. **Freeze right away:** 6 portions into glass containers (leave 1" headspace, lids cracked until frozen). Keep 1 portion in the fridge for T's Mon lunch, eaten within 24 hrs. Freeze the other 7.
6. **Reheat from frozen:** thaw overnight in the fridge or microwave straight from frozen. Eat the same day; don't re-cool.

**Why it works**
- ✅ Low-histamine: fresh meat cooked fast and frozen immediately, no tomato, spinach, vinegar, citrus, fermented foods or long-simmered bone broth
- ✅ Anti-inflammatory: turmeric + pepper, ginger, garlic, olive oil, ~7g fiber
- ✅ TTC: red lentils (folate, iron) + lean protein
- ✅ Wegovy: high protein, easy on the stomach on nausea days
