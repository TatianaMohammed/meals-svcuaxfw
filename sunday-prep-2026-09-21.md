## 🥩 BONE BROTH (START FIRST — 15 min prep, 2–3 hr simmer) 🧡
- [ ] Large pot: chicken thigh trim (save from poach step below) OR 1 lb chicken backs
- [ ] Add: 2 carrots chopped, 1 onion halved, 4 smashed garlic, 2" ginger sliced, 1 tsp turmeric, peppercorns, bay leaves, thyme sprigs, salt, 8 cups water
- [ ] Bring to boil, reduce to bare simmer, cover slightly
- [ ] Simmer 2–3 hrs — skim foam occasionally

*Runs in background — everything else happens while this simmers.*

## 🥔 OVEN SHEET PAN BATCH (400°F, 3 sheets in parallel or rotated)

### Sheet 1: Sweet potato (35 min)
- [ ] 3 large sweet potatoes cubed
- [ ] Toss w/ olive oil + salt + pepper
- [ ] Roast 35 min at 400°F until edges caramelized
- [ ] Portion: Sun D (2 serv) · Tue L (1) · Fri B hash (1) · Sat D (1)

### Sheet 2: Roma-jalapeño salsa base (30 min)
- [ ] 6 romas quartered + 2 jalapeños halved & seeded + 4 garlic cloves + drizzle olive oil + salt
- [ ] Roast 30 min at 400°F
- [ ] Cool, pulse loosely in food processor w/ lime + cilantro (chunky, not smooth)
- [ ] Container → Fri D + snack dip

### Sheet 3: Roasted chicken thighs (35 min, 2 lb)
- [ ] 2 lb thighs (bone-in, skin-on if you have it), thyme + garlic + salt + pepper
- [ ] Roast 35 min at 400°F until 165°F + crispy skin
- [ ] Cool. Slice or leave whole.
- [ ] Portion: Fri L (1 bowl) · Sat L (1 bowl) + snack meat

### Sheet 4 (extra): Butternut squash halves (40 min) — for soup
- [ ] Halve butternut, scoop seeds
- [ ] Cut-side down on sheet, drizzle olive oil, salt
- [ ] Roast 40 min at 400°F until fork-tender
- [ ] Scoop flesh → into soup pot

*Rotate sheets if oven can't fit all 4. Order: butternut + sweet potato first (longest), then thighs + salsa.*

## 🍲 GINGER BUTTERNUT SQUASH SOUP 🧡🧡 (Sun batch, ~30 min after squash done)
- [ ] Sauté 1 diced onion in olive oil, 5 min
- [ ] Add 3 garlic cloves + 2" grated ginger + 1 tsp turmeric + 2 sprigs thyme, 1 min bloom
- [ ] Add roasted butternut flesh + 3 cups bone broth
- [ ] Simmer 10 min
- [ ] Blend w/ immersion blender until silky
- [ ] Stir in ½ cup coconut milk. Salt + pepper.
- [ ] Portion: 4 cups → Tue L + S2 backup container · 2 cups → freezer bags

## 🍲 RED LENTIL DAL 🧡 (Sun D + Mon L, ~35 min)
- [ ] Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in pot. Sauté 1 diced onion 5 min.
- [ ] Add 4 garlic cloves + 2" grated ginger + 1 minced seeded jalapeño + 1 tsp each turmeric/cumin/coriander/garam masala, 1 min bloom

- [ ] Add **1.5 cups rinsed red lentils** + 4 cups water. Boil, reduce, simmer 20–25 min until collapsed.

- [ ] Leftover → Mon L container.
